# Buffett-style memo: COST

## Verdict: own the business

I judge the business, not its price. The code checks pass: ROE is at least 15% in every year, owner earnings are positive in every year, and owner earnings grew from 3,501 to 9,382.

Revenue rose from 226,954 to 303,154, while net income rose from 5,844 to 9,226. ROE remained consistently strong at 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%. The business converted operating cash flow into rising owner earnings even after capital spending, which supports an owner-oriented view of its operating quality.

This is a business-quality judgment using the printed SEC EDGAR facts, not a statement about what the shares are worth or whether to buy them today.

## My decision

I trust the verdict on COST as a disciplined summary of strong five-year operating facts, while recognizing that it does not address valuation, competition, or future change.
