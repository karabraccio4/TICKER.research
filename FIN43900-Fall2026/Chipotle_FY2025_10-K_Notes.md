# Chipotle Mexican Grill: FY2025 Form 10-K Notes

**Filing:** Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed February 4, 2026, accession no. 0001058090-26-000009. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809026000009/cmg-20251231.htm)

## Overview

Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that, together with its subsidiaries, owns and operates Chipotle Mexican Grill restaurants. Its menu includes burritos, burrito bowls, quesadillas, tacos, and salads. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business, p. 4]

The company states that it aims to cultivate a better world through responsibly sourced, classically cooked food made with wholesome ingredients and without artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business, p. 4]

As of December 31, 2025, Chipotle owned 3,938 restaurants in the United States and 104 international restaurants. It also had 14 international partner-operated restaurants. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business, p. 4]

Chipotle manages U.S. operations across 11 regions and aggregates operations into one reportable segment. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business, p. 4]

## Sales trends

Comparable restaurant sales declined 1.7% in 2025, driven by a 2.9% decrease in transactions that was partly offset by a 1.2% increase in average check. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 24-25]

Digital sales represented 36.7% of total food and beverage revenue in 2025. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 24]

Management anticipated approximately flat comparable restaurant sales for 2026. This is management guidance, not a reported result. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 24]

## Restaurant development

Chipotle opened 334 company-owned restaurants in 2025, including 257 with a Chipotlane. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 24]

For 2026, management expected to open approximately 350 to 370 restaurants, including 10 to 15 international partner-operated restaurants. This is management guidance, not a reported result. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 24]

## Quarterly financial data and seasonality

Profitability can fluctuate by quarter. Historically, average daily restaurant sales and net income have been lower in the first and fourth quarters, in part because of the holiday season and winter weather. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]

Restaurants near colleges and universities generally do more business during the academic year. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]

Seasonal patterns can be moderated or outweighed by unexpected publicity, disease outbreaks, inflation and consumer sentiment, food or packaging-cost changes, holiday timing, menu-price increases, promotional activity, and other marketing initiatives. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]

The number of trading days in a quarter can affect quarterly results, although changes in trading days do not have a significant impact on an annual basis. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]

Quarterly results can also be affected by stock-based compensation and related tax impacts, litigation and settlement costs, impairment charges, non-operating costs, marketing timing, restaurant openings and closures. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]

New restaurants typically have higher operating costs shortly after opening because of opening expenses and operating inefficiencies in the first months of operation. Therefore, one quarter's results are not necessarily indicative of another quarter's or a full year's results. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 28]
