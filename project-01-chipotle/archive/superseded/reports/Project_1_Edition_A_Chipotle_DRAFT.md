## Initial thesis: watch / defer

I would **watch / defer**, rather than initiate immediately. The filing reports revenue growth and continued unit expansion, as well as no outstanding revolver borrowings. However, comparable restaurant sales declined in 2025 because transactions fell. The filing alone does not establish that the market price at the valuation date provides a sufficient margin of safety. Further work should test the durability of comparable-sales growth, restaurant economics, and the valuation implied by the share price.

## Known evidence from the filing

- **Cash and cash equivalents:** $350.545 million at December 31, 2025 (balance-sheet amount: 350,545, in thousands).
- **Debt:** $0 outstanding under the $500.0 million revolving credit facility at December 31, 2025. The unused facility is borrowing capacity, not debt to deduct in the bridge.
- **Diluted share evidence:** 1,342.616 million diluted weighted-average common shares for FY2025 (reported as 1,342,616, in thousands). This includes 5.280 million dilutive stock awards above basic weighted-average shares.
- **Selected operating-value driver - comparable restaurant sales:** comparable restaurant sales decreased 1.7% in FY2025. The decline reflected a 2.9% decrease in transactions, partly offset by a 1.2% increase in average check. This is consequential because management identifies comparable restaurant sales as a key operating metric that drives financial results and its long-term growth model.
- **Supporting context:** Chipotle opened 334 company-owned restaurants in 2025, including 257 with a Chipotlane, and ended the year with 4,042 company-owned restaurants.

Sources: [Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809026000009/cmg-20251231.htm), Consolidated Balance Sheets (p. 38), MD&A (pp. 24-25), Note 10 - Earnings Per Share (p. 60), and Note 12 - Debt (p. 62).

## Consequential assumptions

- Cash and cash equivalents are treated as available to equity holders in the synthetic bridge. This is a simplifying assumption, not proof that all cash is excess to operating needs.
- Zero revolver borrowings represent debt outstanding at the reporting date; the undrawn facility is financing capacity rather than a liability.
- FY2025 diluted weighted-average shares are appropriate only for a valuation aligned to FY2025. A later valuation date would require an updated share count and consideration of awards.
- The 2025 comparable-sales decline is evidence of a risk to the thesis, not proof that sales will decline permanently.

## Unknowns to resolve
