## Investment view

The central outstanding question is whether transaction growth and unit economics can support the earnings growth embedded in the market valuation. [UNSOURCED]

## Operating performance and growth

Comparable restaurant sales declined 1.7% in fiscal 2025. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, pp. 24–25]

The decline reflected a 2.9% decrease in transactions, partly offset by a 1.2% increase in average check. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, pp. 24–25]

Chipotle opened 334 company-owned restaurants during 2025 and operated 4,042 company-owned restaurants at year-end. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, p. 25]

The comparable-sales decline is treated as a meaningful risk indicator, rather than proof of a permanent decline. [UNSOURCED]

## Liquidity, debt, and share count

Cash and cash equivalents were $350.545 million at December 31, 2025 ($350,545 thousand as reported). [FY2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 38]

Chipotle had no borrowings outstanding under its $500.0 million revolving credit facility at December 31, 2025. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 12—Debt, p. 62]

The undrawn revolver is financing capacity, not debt to deduct from enterprise value. [UNSOURCED]

Fiscal 2025 diluted weighted-average common shares were 1,342.616 million (1,342,616 thousand as reported). [FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 10—Earnings Per Share, p. 60]

Dilutive stock awards exceeded basic weighted-average shares by 5.280 million. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Note 10—Earnings Per Share, p. 60]

## What remains to be tested

The report does not provide a sourced closing share price or market capitalization for the valuation date. [UNSOURCED]

The report does not establish the amount of cash that is excess rather than operational. [UNSOURCED]

The ability to restore transaction growth, sustain comparable-sales growth, and earn attractive returns on new restaurants and Chipotlanes remains unestablished. [UNSOURCED]

Further analysis should test unit economics, including development costs, restaurant-level margins, lease obligations, and incremental returns; develop comparable-sales, transaction, unit-opening, and operating-margin scenarios; and include a downside case with continuing transaction pressure.
