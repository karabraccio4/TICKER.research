## Partner challenge and response

**Partner:** Why did you choose 9.0% FY2026 revenue growth when management only guided comparable restaurant sales to be about flat?

I chose 9.0% because the 350–370 planned company-owned openings add growth even with flat sales at existing restaurants, but I rounded the implied unit-driven growth down because openings contribute for only part of the year. I would lower this assumption if openings fall below guidance or comparable sales decline, and raise it if openings are achieved and comparable sales turn positive.

## Apple peer review (hypothetical example)

*Because I do not have my partner's actual Apple assumption table, this example assumes that their judgment is 6.0% FY2026 revenue growth.*

**My question to my Apple partner:** I saw that you forecast 6.0% FY2026 revenue growth; how does that assumption separate growth from iPhone and other products from growth in Services, and what evidence would make you revise it?

**My attack:** Your 6.0% growth judgment repeats Apple's FY2025 total-sales growth without showing whether the higher-growth Services business is large enough to offset a slower iPhone upgrade cycle, weaker Wearables sales, or geographic pressure. Since Services grew 14% in FY2025 while total sales grew 6%, you should show the product-versus-Services revenue mix or run a sensitivity case instead of assuming that last year's total rate will automatically continue.

**Partner's recorded answer:** I used 6.0% as a base case because it matches FY2025 total-sales growth and is supported by 14% Services growth, which is both recurring and higher margin than products. I would reduce the forecast if iPhone demand, China sales, or Services growth weakens, and increase it only if the next product cycle and Services results support growth above the base case.

## Organic growth — partner explanation

Organic growth is growth generated by the existing business rather than by acquisitions, divestitures, or other changes to the reporting perimeter. For CMG, the closest MD&A measure is **comparable restaurant sales**: the period-over-period revenue change for company-owned restaurants that have operated for at least 13 full calendar months; it was 7.9% in FY2023, 7.4% in FY2024, and (1.7%) in FY2025.

The video carries 1.8% for ABG because that is the organic growth assumption for the dealerships already in the base, while the reported 4.7% growth includes growth from changes outside that comparable base, such as acquired operations or other portfolio changes. Using 4.7% as the organic forecast would double-count growth that does not come from the existing dealerships.
