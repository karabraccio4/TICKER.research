# Project 1 - Edition A
## Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc. (NYSE: CMG)

**Recommendation:** Watch / defer  
**Intended user:** Investment Committee  
**Valuation date:** December 31, 2025  
**Prepared from:** FY2025 Form 10-K

## Decision

The Investment Committee should defer initiating a position pending completion of valuation work. Chipotle combines revenue growth, continued restaurant expansion, substantial liquidity, and no outstanding revolver borrowings. However, comparable restaurant sales declined during FY2025 as transactions declined, and the Form 10-K alone does not demonstrate that the market price at the valuation date offers an adequate margin of safety. The central question for further work is whether transaction growth and unit economics can support the earnings growth embedded in the market valuation.

## Sensitivity grid and reverse DCF

The sensitivity grid holds starting FCFF, cash, debt, diluted shares, and the five explicit FCFF growth rates fixed at the base case. Value falls as WACC rises and increases as terminal growth rises.

| 10% | $16.52 | $18.28 | $20.63 |
| 11% | $14.68 | $16.00 | $17.69 |

For the reverse DCF, the target price is the current CMG price of $36.03. The model applies one uniform shift to all five base explicit FCFF growth rates (5.0%, 7.0%, 7.0%, 6.0%, and 5.0%) while holding starting FCFF ($1,447.590m), WACC (8.5%), terminal growth (2.5%), cash ($350.545m), debt ($0), and diluted shares (1,342.616m) fixed. Bisection over the required -5.0% to +10.0% shift bracket found **no solution in this bracket**: the $36.03 target is not reachable without expanding the prescribed search range or changing another input. This is an assumption check, not proof of mispricing.

## Conditional call

**Watch / defer.** Initiate only if CMG's market price falls to or below the base-case value of about $21.69 per share, or if new, sourced evidence justifies a stronger FCFF growth path. Otherwise, defer. **Monitor:** comparable restaurant sales, especially transaction growth and restaurant-level operating margin in the next quarterly filing.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Cash and cash equivalents are assumed to be available to equity holders in the DCF equity bridge. This is a simplifying valuation assumption; further work must determine what portion is needed for operations.
2. Zero revolver borrowings represent debt outstanding at the reporting date. The $500.0 million undrawn facility is not a debt balance.
3. FY2025 diluted weighted-average shares are used because the valuation date is aligned with FY2025. A later valuation date would require an updated share count and review of stock awards.
4. The FY2025 comparable-sales decline is treated as a meaningful risk indicator, not as proof of a permanent decline.

## Unknowns

- The CMG closing share price and market capitalization applicable to the selected valuation date.
- The portion of reported cash that is excess rather than operational.
- The ability to restore transaction growth and sustain comparable-sales growth.
- New-restaurant and Chipotlane investment requirements, unit economics, and incremental returns.
- Intrinsic value under conservative, base, and upside cases relative to the market value.

## Research plan

1. Revisit the DCF assumptions and test conservative, base, and upside cases.
2. Obtain the valuation-date closing price and calculate market capitalization using the appropriate diluted share convention.
3. Analyze unit economics, including development costs, restaurant-level margins, lease obligations, and returns on new restaurants.
4. Develop scenarios for comparable sales, transaction recovery, unit openings, and operating margin; include a downside case with continued transaction pressure.
5. Value the scenarios and identify the assumption with the greatest effect on the investment conclusion.
